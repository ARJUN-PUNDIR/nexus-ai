# Detailed Explanation

Narendra Modi is an Indian politician and government official who rose to become a senior leader of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP). He was born on September 17, 1950 in Vadnagar, India. Prior to becoming Prime Minister, Modi served as chief minister (head of government) of Gujarat state in western India from 2001 to 2014. In 2014, he led his party to victory in elections to the Lok Sabha (lower chamber of the Indian parliament), after which he was sworn in as prime minister of India.

# Conclusion

Narendra Modi has been a prominent political figure in India, serving as chief minister of Gujarat before becoming the country's Prime Minister in 2014 after leading his party to electoral success.
